How to play Gravity Wells

Gravity Wells: Cargo Puzzle is a sliding block puzzle with a twist: you don't move one block, you tilt the entire board. Here is everything you need to know, from the first swipe to three stars.

The one rule

Swipe in any direction and the whole cargo hold tilts that way. Every crate slides at once until it hits a wall or another crate. A crate whose arrow points the way you swiped shoots out through the airlock when its path is clear; the others pack up against the walls.

That's it. The puzzle is setting the hold up so that one swipe sends as many crates as possible flying out in a single cascade.

Stars and par

Each hold is a logic puzzle with a tilt budget. Clear it in the fewest tilts to earn all three stars. There's no timer, and restarting is always free, so you can experiment as much as you like.

Mechanics you'll meet

New mechanics arrive one at a time as you salvage 21 derelict spaceships, from the little Tug to the Last Light. Bolt, the salvage drone, teaches each one by doing, not by reading.

  • Bulkhead gates: the one thing you tap. Tap to flip them open or shut.
  • Spinner crates: they turn with every swipe, so their arrow keeps changing.
  • Rotator plates
  • Linked pairs: they leave together.
  • Heavy anchors
  • Big double crates
  • Fragile glass
  • Grease pads
  • Clamps
  • Colour-locked airlocks

Tips for three stars

  • Read the arrows first. Before you swipe, look at which way most arrows point. That's usually where your big cascade will go.
  • Use the walls. Crates that can't leave yet pack against the walls. A setup swipe that lines them up often makes the next swipe clear the hold.
  • Think one swipe ahead. Every swipe moves everything, so ask where each crate will end up, not just the one you care about.
  • Stuck? Undo a tilt, ask for a hint, or blast a crate out of the way.
  • Keep the streak. Clearing holds in a row earns growing bonus coins, and star chests reward collecting stars.

Other ways to play

  • Daily Puzzle with bonus coins and a 7-day login calendar.
  • Salvage Run: endless cargo, endless choices, for when you just want to swipe.
  • Looks: Candy, Neon, Ice and 80s Retro are cosmetic only.
  • Accessibility: colour-blind symbols, Reduced motion and adjustable swipe sensitivity.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes: free to play, with ads and optional in-app purchases. You'll never see an ad right after a failed attempt, reward videos are always your choice, and a one-time purchase removes the other ads.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Play offline, anywhere, with no account.

How many levels?

1,000 levels across 21 spaceships, plus the Daily Puzzle and Salvage Run.

Can I play on iPhone in the EU?

Not yet. The App Store version is available outside the EU. On Android, Google Play has it everywhere, including the EU.

I like arrow puzzles, unblock games and sorting games. Will I like this?

Then you'll feel at home here.
