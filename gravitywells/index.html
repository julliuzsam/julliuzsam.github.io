Easy to start. Hard to put down.

Tilt, don't push

You don't move one block. Every swipe tilts the entire board. Crates whose arrows point the way you tilted shoot out through the airlock, and the rest pack against the walls.

Set up the big clear

Each hold is a logic puzzle with a tilt budget. Plan your moves, then clear the hold in a cascade and earn all three stars.

Always something new

Bulkhead gates you tap to flip, spinners, rotator plates, linked pairs, anchors, big crates, fragile glass, grease pads, clamps and colour-locked airlocks.

Learn in a minute

Bolt the salvage drone teaches you by doing, not by reading. Stuck? Undo a tilt, ask for a hint, or blast a crate out of the way.

Reasons to come back

Win streaks, a Daily Puzzle, a 7-day login calendar, star chests and Salvage Run, an endless mode for when you just want to swipe.

Relaxed by design

No timers, and restart is always free. Plays offline with colour-blind symbols, a Reduced motion setting and adjustable swipe sensitivity.
