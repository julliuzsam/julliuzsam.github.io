ONE LINE
One swipe tilts the whole cargo hold, and every crate slides at once.

SHORT
Gravity Wells is a tilt puzzle for phones. Instead of moving single blocks, you tilt the whole board: crates pointing the way you swiped fly out through the airlock, and the rest pack against the walls. There are 1,000 levels across 21 derelict spaceships, with no timers, free restarts and offline play.

LONG
Gravity Wells turns the sliding-block puzzle on its side. Every swipe tilts the entire cargo hold, so every crate moves at once. A crate escapes only when its arrow points the way you tilted, the airlock is open and its path is clear. The challenge is to set up the board so a single swipe sends five or six crates flying in a cascade.

Players salvage 21 derelict ships across 1,000 levels. Bolt, a small salvage drone, teaches the rules by doing, and every new mechanic opens with a live demo card and a guided first board. New mechanics arrive steadily: bulkhead gates you tap to flip, spinner crates, rotator plates, linked pairs, heavy anchors, big double crates, fragile glass, grease pads, clamps and colour-locked airlocks. Win streaks, a Daily Puzzle, star chests and an endless Salvage Run give reasons to come back.

The game is built to be relaxed and accessible: no timers, restart is always free, it plays offline, and it includes colour-blind symbols, a Reduced-motion option and adjustable swipe sensitivity.
